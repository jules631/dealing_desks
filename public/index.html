The problem

CPQ tools start with fields. Your deal starts with a conversation.

Every AE has been there: a 40-minute call full of budget signals, timeline pressure, and competitive context — followed by an hour of manually translating that into CPQ fields. The translation is lossy by design. The quote comes out misconfigured, the justification is boilerplate, and deal desk sends it back.

The cost isn't just the time. It's the deals that slip because the internal approval process took longer than the prospect's decision window.

The opportunity

The conversation already contains everything you need to price and justify the deal.

Company size. Budget signals. Timeline pressure. Competitive context. Decision authority. It's all there — in the call notes, the email thread, the Slack message. Deal Desk AI reads the conversation and extracts structured deal intelligence, recommends a product configuration, flags discount risk, and drafts a justification grounded in what was actually said.

No form-filling. No re-encoding. The conversation is the source of truth.

How it works

5 steps
01
Paste the conversation

Drop in raw call notes, an email thread, a Slack export — anything. The AI reads unstructured text, not form fields.

02
Claude extracts deal signals

Every signal is labeled Explicit or Implied — distinguishing what the prospect said directly from what can be inferred.

03
Get a configured quote

Product, pricing model, quantity, and applicable volume discounts are recommended based on the conversation signals and your catalog rules.

04
Review discount risk

Green, amber, or red — the risk assessment tells you where you stand against policy before you commit, including VP approval flags.

05
Submit with a grounded justification

The approval justification cites specific signals from the conversation. Optionally run a deal desk simulation to stress-test it first.
