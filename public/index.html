How it works

01
Paste the conversation
Email threads, call notes, Slack messages — any raw text from the prospect interaction. No formatting required.
02
AI extracts signals
Claude reads the conversation and extracts company context, budget signals, timeline pressure, objections, and discount expectations. Each signal is marked Explicit or Implied.
03
Get a quote configuration
The AI maps signals to the product catalog and recommends seat vs. consumption pricing, volume tier, and contract term — with reasoning grounded in the conversation.
04
Copy the justification
A deal desk–ready approval justification is drafted in the AE's voice — specific, grounded, and ready to submit. One click to copy.

What you get

Signal extraction
Company size, industry, tech stack, budget signals, timeline pressure, objections, and decision-maker signals — all pulled from raw conversation text. Each signal is confidence-rated.
Quote configuration
Product recommendations mapped to conversation signals. Seat vs. consumption pricing recommendation with explicit reasoning. Volume discount tiers applied automatically.
Discount risk flag
Before you commit to a number, see the gap between what the prospect implied and what policy allows. Green / amber / red rating with a plain-language explanation.
Approval justification draft
The justification an AE submits to deal desk — grounded in specific signals from the conversation. Confident, specific, business-justified. Not boilerplate.
Deal desk simulation
Run a second AI pass — Claude acts as a skeptical deal desk analyst and returns the 2–3 pushback questions the AE should be ready for before submitting.
Conversation-first
Existing CPQ tools are form-first. They start with fields. This starts with the conversation — the actual source of truth for every deal.
